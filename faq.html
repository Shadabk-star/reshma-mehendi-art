1. How Early Should I Arrive For My Appointment?

Please arrive 10-15 minutes before your scheduled time so we can prepare and discuss your design. Late arrivals may result in a shortened session to accommodate other bookings.

2. How Long Does Mehendi Take To Apply?

Depending on the design, simple patterns take 15-30 minutes, while bridal or full-hand designs may take 2-3 hours.

3. Is There Anything I Should Bring?

No preparation needed on your end — just come with clean, oil-free skin. If you have a specific design in mind, feel free to bring reference photos.

4. Can I Choose My Own Design?

Yes, you can choose your own design or let our artists create a custom design for you. Bringing reference photos can help us understand your preferences better.

5. Do You Offer Home Service?

Yes, we offer home service for your convenience.Please contact us to check availability and any additional requirements.
